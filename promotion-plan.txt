Assignment 5 – Social Media Promotion Plan

Business Name: Rolls Bakery & Specialty Coffee Shop

Business Type: Fictional Bakery and Specialty Coffee Shop

1. What product or service is being promoted?

Rolls Bakery & Specialty Coffee Shop offers freshly baked bread, croissants, pastries, cakes, cookies, and premium specialty coffee. The bakery focuses on high-quality ingredients, fresh daily products, affordable prices, and a warm, welcoming atmosphere. Customers can enjoy their favourite baked goods and beverages in a comfortable café environment or purchase them for takeaway.

2. Who is the target audience?

The primary target audience includes college and university students, office workers, and local residents aged 18–45 who enjoy fresh baked goods and specialty coffee.

Students are attracted to affordable coffee, snacks, and a comfortable environment for studying or meeting friends. Office workers are interested in convenient breakfast options, quick coffee breaks, and takeaway products. Local residents and families are potential customers looking for fresh pastries, desserts, and a relaxing neighbourhood café.

3. Which social media platforms are most suitable?

Instagram: Ideal for sharing high-quality photographs, Reels, and Stories featuring fresh pastries, specialty coffee, seasonal products, and behind-the-scenes baking.

Facebook: Suitable for connecting with local residents, promoting special offers, sharing business updates, and announcing seasonal promotions.

TikTok: Effective for reaching younger customers through short, engaging videos showing pastry preparation, coffee-making techniques, and new menu items.

4. Proposed Hashtags

#RollsBakery
#FreshlyBakedDaily
#SpecialtyCoffee
#BakeryAndCoffee
#FreshPastries

5. Promotional Strategy (2–3 sentences)

Rolls Bakery & Specialty Coffee Shop will use Instagram and TikTok to attract customers through appealing food photography, short baking videos, and engaging content highlighting freshly prepared pastries and specialty coffee. Facebook will support local community engagement by promoting seasonal offers, special discounts, and new product announcements. The overall strategy will focus on increasing brand awareness, attracting new customers, and encouraging repeat visits through consistent social media activity and visually appealing promotions.
